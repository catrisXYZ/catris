# Cats fall. Lines clear. Tax feeds the litter.

**Subtitle:** Tetris with cats, on Robinhood Chain, launching on letscash.fun. Fifteen minutes. One well. Several uncooperative roommates.

---

There are seven kinds of cat.

Red is a long boy. He arrives horizontal, looks you in the eye, and dares you to find a hole that is exactly four cells wide. Blue is a square. Blue has never had a thought that lasted more than one rotation. Green is a T. Green believes in balance. Gold and orange are the L twins — same bones, opposite politics. Magenta and cyan are the ones that S and Z their way into your evening.

They are not blocks. They are cats. The ears hang off the cell. The tails refuse to stay inside the grid. This is not a bug. This is the original drawing, from a tiny js13k game, now living on a chain that did not exist when the first kitten was inked.

Welcome to **Catris**.

## The well

Ten across. Twenty down. Two secret rows above the lip where the next catastrophe spawns. Guideline gravity, DAS, ARR, a ghost so you can see the crime before you commit it. Hold is allowed. Pause is allowed. Pretending you meant to do that is allowed.

Then a black cat shows up.

Still will not rotate. He has seen your plan. He does not respect it.

Lazy will not slide. DAS means nothing to him. He is a brick with whiskers.

Pounce does not wait for the lock delay. Pounce is already on the floor. You were still thinking.

These are not power-ups. They are personality. The well is a small apartment and you are the landlord who cannot evict anyone.

## The clock

Every **fifteen minutes** the well holds an election.

Highest score in that window takes **60%** of the pot. Holders get a merkle drip of **30%**. Team takes **10%**, which is how the litter box stays funded and the lights stay on.

The pot is not minted from vibes. It is the **3% trade tax** on $CATRIS, launched on [letscash.fun](https://letscash.fun/launch) as a Uniswap v4 pool on **Robinhood Chain**. No bonding curve. No “we will migrate later.” The fee is the product.

The important bit: the creator stream does not go to a wallet someone can empty at 3 a.m. It goes to a **vault contract**. The vault is a box. Boxes do not have weekend plans.

## How a run becomes a prize

1. You stack cats. You clear lines. You swear at Pounce.
2. The litter fills. You post the score to the arena — optionally signed on-chain.
3. The epoch closes. A keeper who cannot move funds, only close clocks, rings the bell.
4. First place is paid. Holders can claim the drip. The next fifteen minutes starts like nothing happened, which is very cat of it.

If you did not win, you still played a real game. That is the whole pitch. The token is the meter on the arcade cabinet. The cabinet is the point.
